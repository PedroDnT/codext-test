    How to play

    A new puzzle unlocks every day. Study the GDP treemap and guess which country it represents. You have ten attempts.

    • Each guess must be a valid country from the list.
    • After every incorrect guess you'll learn how far away you are and which direction to travel to reach the target country.
    • Keep track of your streak — your progress is saved for the day on this device.
